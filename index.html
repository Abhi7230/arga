Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to shoot professionally?

No. Phone clips are perfect. Talk to the camera, show your products or your space. Arga picks the best moments and cuts them into reels.

Which platforms do you post to?

Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts to start. More platforms will follow.

Will it post something I don't like?

Not unless you turn on autopost. By default every reel comes to you on WhatsApp first and goes out only after your tap.

Is the video AI-generated?

No. Arga edits your own footage. AI helps choose the moments, cut, caption and schedule; your business stays real.

How does the free trial work?

Send your clips on WhatsApp and within 24 hours you get 3 finished reels with captions. One trial per business (one Instagram account and one WhatsApp number). Trial reels end with a small "Made with Arga" card; paid plans don't. Like them? Continue on a monthly plan. If not, you keep the reels.
