Questions

Do I need to shoot professionally?

No. Phone clips are fine. Talking to the camera, your products, your space: Arga picks the best moments and cuts them into reels.

Which platforms do you post to?

Instagram (Reels) and YouTube Shorts to start. More platforms will follow.

Will it post something I don't like?

Not unless you choose autopost. By default every reel comes to you on WhatsApp first, and you approve it with a tap.

What happens after the free week?

If you like it, you continue on a monthly plan. If not, you stop, and you keep the reels.
