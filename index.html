Arga Expert evaluation for AI video

The people judging AI video
should be filmmakers.

Arga puts vetted Indian film, VFX and editing professionals in front of AI-generated video. They compare clips in pairs and write timestamped, craft-specific reasons — where the camera move breaks, where light direction flips between cuts, why a shot doesn't hold.

Every batch is gold-checked and inter-rater agreement is tracked, so the preference data that comes out is something a model team can actually train and benchmark on.

How a pilot runs

1

You send clips

Model outputs, paired however you need — A/B, ablations, or checkpoint comparisons.

2

Experts rate them

Working film, VFX and edit professionals score against a structured craft rubric and leave timestamped notes.

3

You get clean data

Labelled pairs with reasons, gold-pair quality checks, and Cohen's kappa agreement per rater.

What makes it different

  • Craft, not vibes. Raters are people who light, shoot and cut for a living — so the feedback names the specific failure, not just "looks worse".
  • Quality you can audit. Seeded gold pairs catch low-effort raters; agreement is measured, not assumed.
  • India's talent depth. One of the world's largest working film and VFX communities, at a fraction of US evaluation cost.
  • Built for handoff. Exports are structured datasets, not spreadsheets someone has to clean up.
