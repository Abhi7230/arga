How it works

From clip to posted reel.

You keep running your business. Arga turns what you already shoot on your phone into a steady stream of reels, and posts them for you.

1

Connect your accounts once

Link Instagram and YouTube. Arga posts only to the accounts you connect.

2

Send your clips

On WhatsApp, whenever you have them: a new product, a busy evening at your café, a transformation at your gym, you explaining something. Rough, unedited phone clips are fine.

3

Arga picks the moments

It listens to what's said, finds the strongest seconds, removes the dead air and puts the most interesting moment first, so people stop scrolling.

4

It finishes the reel

Vertical framing, clean cuts, readable captions on screen, a colour pass, and music where it fits.

5

It writes the post

A caption in your language (Hinglish, Hindi or English), hashtags, and a posting time chosen for your audience, around Indian festivals and occasions.

6

You approve, or it autoposts

Each reel arrives on WhatsApp. Tap to approve, ask for a change, or skip it. With autopost on, finished reels go out on schedule by themselves.
