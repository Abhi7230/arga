How Arga Autopilot works

You keep running your business. Arga turns what you already shoot on your phone into a steady stream of reels, and posts them for you.

From clip to posted reel

  1. Connect your accounts once. Link Instagram and YouTube. Arga posts only to the accounts you connect.
  2. Send your clips. On WhatsApp, whenever you have them: a new product, a busy evening at your café, a client transformation at your gym, you explaining something. Rough, unedited phone clips are fine.
  3. Arga picks the moments. It listens to what's said, finds the strongest seconds, removes the dead air and puts the most interesting moment first so people stop scrolling.
  4. It finishes the reel. Vertical framing, clean cuts, readable captions on screen, a colour pass, and music where it fits.
  5. It writes the post. A caption in your language (Hinglish, Hindi or English), hashtags, and a posting time chosen for your audience, around Indian festivals and occasions.
  6. You approve, or it autoposts. Each reel arrives on WhatsApp. Tap to approve, ask for a change, or skip it. With autopost on, finished reels go out on schedule by themselves.

What we never do

  • Make fake people, fake reviews or fake claims about your business.
  • Use stock footage and pass it off as yours.
  • Post anything you haven't approved, unless you switch on autopost.
  • Share or sell your footage. Your clips are used only to make your posts.

What you need

  • An Instagram business or creator account and/or a YouTube channel.
  • A phone that shoots video, and WhatsApp.
  • A minute a day to approve.
